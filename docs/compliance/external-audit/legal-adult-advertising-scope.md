# External legal review: adult platform and advertising model

This review must be performed by qualified Brazilian counsel familiar with
internet platforms, privacy/data protection, consumer law, advertising and
adult-content businesses.

## Adult platform model

Counsel should review, at minimum:

- eligibility for an adult-only service and the age-assurance model;
- legal sufficiency and proportionality of age verification/reverification;
- terms governing adult consensual content;
- prohibited-content definitions;
- moderation, notice, appeal and emergency-removal procedures;
- child/adolescent protection obligations;
- preservation and lawful disclosure of evidence/data;
- provider obligations under the Marco Civil da Internet and later applicable
  regulations;
- LGPD controller/operator roles, sensitive data and age-assurance evidence;
- consumer-law implications of subscriptions, cancellation, refunds and
  recurring charging;
- processor/acquirer restrictions for the adult segment;
- corporate/CNAE/tax/contractual requirements relevant to launch.

## Advertising / Promotion/Boost

Counsel should separately review:

- whether paid placement is clearly distinguishable from organic ranking;
- required disclosure such as "Patrocinado";
- advertiser eligibility and prohibited advertisers/categories;
- substantiation of advertising claims;
- targeting restrictions, especially use of sensitive data or inferred sexual
  preferences;
- frequency caps and anti-manipulation controls;
- influencer/testimonial/endorsement treatment where applicable;
- consumer transparency around billing, duration and cancellation of boosts;
- compatibility with the CONAR Code and applicable category-specific rules;
- contractual allocation of responsibility between platform and advertiser.

## Brazil-specific sources to confirm at review time

The legal reviewer must confirm the current versions and applicability of,
among others:

- Lei 13.709/2018 (LGPD) and ANPD regulations/guidance;
- Lei 12.965/2014 (Marco Civil da Internet) and regulations;
- Lei 8.069/1990 (ECA), including provisions addressing sexual/pornographic
  material involving children or adolescents;
- Lei 15.211/2025 and subsequent regulation on protection of children and
  adolescents in digital environments, when applicable;
- current CONAR Code and relevant decisions/guidance;
- consumer and payment rules applicable to subscriptions and advertising.

The deliverable must identify any launch blocker explicitly and distinguish
mandatory law, regulatory interpretation, self-regulation and contractual risk.
